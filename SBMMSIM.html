How the simulator itself works (plain English)
  • Every player has a hidden "true skill" from 1 to 100. Nobody, including the matchmaker, can see it directly.
  • In each match, your kills and deaths depend on how your true skill (plus a little good-day or bad-day luck, minus a small penalty for high ping) compares with the enemy team. Beat a weaker team and your K/D goes up. Face a stronger team and it goes down.
  • The matchmaker cannot see true skill. It builds lobbies using what it can see: your skill bracket, your MMR and your connection. How much weight each gets depends on the mode.
  • MMR (modes 2, 3 and 4) is updated after every match: did you do better or worse than the system expected against that lobby? The "MMR Aggressiveness" slider controls how far one match can move it.
  • Skill brackets only shift slowly: once every 20 matches, and only if your average K/D over those 20 was well above or below 1.0.
  • All randomness comes from the seed in the toolbar, so the same seed always gives the same players and the same results. Handy for recording.

Data and formulas based on Activision's published whitepapers (2024), the Activision matchmaking patent (US 10,322,351), TrueSkill research by Microsoft Research, and publicly available information from Charlie Olson (former Raven Software, MMR algorithm creator).

This is a simulation for educational purposes. It does not use Activision's proprietary source code.
